About Me

Web Developer

Hi, I'm Praise — I build for the web.

I'm a web developer and digital creator based in Masvingo, Zimbabwe. I hold Level 4 and Level 5 Diplomas in Computing and Information Systems from Pace College Harare, and I'm currently studying toward my Level 6.

My work spans both static and dynamic websites — from clean, fast marketing sites to full PHP & MySQL platforms like To-Linka. I've delivered real client projects including schools, finance firms, and security companies — sites that are responsive, SEO-optimised, and built to perform.

Beyond the code, I'm an aspiring photographer — chasing light, framing moments, and learning to tell stories without a single line of code. It's a craft I'm actively growing, and one I plan to take seriously as I evolve as a creator.

And yes — the purple you see everywhere? That's not an accident. Purple is my colour. It's in the design, the gradients, the glow. Consider it a signature.
